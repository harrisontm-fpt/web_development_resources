# Session 05 workshop — Proposal clinic and weekly plan

[Lecture](lecture.md) · [Session index](../README.md)

**60-minute workshop + 60-minute project block · Mandatory proposal, ungraded**

## Before you start

Your proposal must be accessible before class. Bring the board, wireframes, scope exclusions, risks, and members' weekly availability. Check that the lecturer can open linked resources.

## Clinic rotation (0–45 minutes)

| Time | Team with lecturer | Other teams |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 | A | Split an essential journey into tasks |
| 15–30 | B | Agree request/response examples and dependencies |
| 30–45 | C | Review capacity and definition of done |

All members attend their clinic. Use approximately three minutes for users/problem, four for MVP/design, four for feasibility/risks, and four to agree actions.

Explain:

1. What the user needs and what is excluded.
2. Which essential journey you will build first.
3. How the UI, API, and database will cooperate.
4. Who will implement, review, and integrate it.
5. What assumption you most need to check.

Record feedback, owner, and due date in the [proposal action table](../../project_proposal.md).

## Work while waiting

Select the first connected journey and write small tasks supporting it. Create a request/response example in the repository. Agree field names and error/empty behaviour relevant to that journey.

Assign owners and reviewers, note dependencies, and estimate work against actual availability. Include integration and setup documentation. A teammate should be able to start a Ready card without guessing its finish condition.

## Shared feedback (45–60 minutes)

Each team reports one scope or feasibility decision in no more than two minutes. Discuss common issues for the remaining time. Revise your weekly objective and select one improvement to how your team plans work.

## Project block (60 minutes)

Use 5 minutes to choose an action, 45 implementing proposal revisions and initial tasks, and 10 updating evidence. Keep the original proposal date and record changes rather than disguising earlier assumptions as confirmed facts.

## Evidence to keep

- [ ] Accessible proposal and linked wireframes/backlog.
- [ ] Clinic feedback with owners and deadlines.
- [ ] One demonstrable weekly objective.
- [ ] Small tasks including integration and review.
- [ ] An initial API example and data-relationship sketch.
- [ ] Capacity and dependencies considered.

**If the proposal is incomplete:** show what exists, identify the missing decisions, and agree a completion action with the lecturer. Do not spend the review pretending missing requirements are resolved.
**If finished early:** have a member who did not write the API example explain the fields and expected screen.
**Before session 6:** bring your WSK Express/SQL environment and the simplest useful endpoint to implement.
