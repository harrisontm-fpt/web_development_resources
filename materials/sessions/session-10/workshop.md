# Session 10 workshop — Observe and improve usability

[Lecture](lecture.md) · [Session index](../README.md)

**60-minute workshop + 60-minute project block · Ungraded MVP/usability check**

## Before you start

Run the application with representative development/demo data. Prepare the essential journeys, wireframes, and the [UX observation table](../../ux_workbook.md). Assign facilitator, observer, and tester roles.

## Step 1 — Prepare neutral tasks (0–10 minutes)

Write two or three tasks tied to acceptance criteria. Give context and a goal without naming controls. Include a form or alternative state if relevant.

Specify the starting screen and success condition. Check the app is usable by a visiting tester and give only the information a real user would reasonably have.

## Step 2 — Observe another person (10–40 minutes)

Use A sends a tester to B, B to C, C to A. The two remaining members facilitate and record. Divide 30 minutes into approximately:

- 15 minutes attempting the ordinary tasks.
- 10 minutes checking a narrow layout and keyboard interaction.
- 5 minutes exploring errors or recovery.

Ask the tester to explain expectations. Do not coach them through the task. If they get stuck, record the difficulty before helping. Use the browser's responsive view for layout checks and actual keyboard input for interaction checks.

Record task, device/viewport, outcome, observed difficulty, and interpretation. Label this peer testing; do not describe classmates as target-user research if they are not representative.

## Step 3 — Prioritise and revise (40–60 minutes)

Share observations with your full team. Rank findings:

1. Blocks an essential task.
2. Makes an essential task difficult or confusing.
3. Cosmetic or optional improvement.

Choose the highest-impact fix that fits your available time. Link it to a Trello card with an owner and a retest task. Compare current screens with wireframes and record significant design changes.

## Project block (60 minutes)

Use 5 minutes to select work, 45 to fix and retest, and 10 to update evidence. At least one changed interaction should be retried by someone other than its implementer. Record what improved and what remains uncertain.

## Evidence to keep

- [ ] Neutral tasks and success conditions.
- [ ] Observation notes, including tester type and context.
- [ ] Mobile and keyboard findings.
- [ ] Prioritised fixes tied to observed problems.
- [ ] Before/after or design-decision evidence.
- [ ] Retest result for the priority fix.
- [ ] Updated MVP status and remaining requirements.

**If the app is blocked technically:** observe any working journey and test the remaining interaction through its prototype, clearly distinguishing prototype results from implementation results. Add the technical blocker to the board.
**If no problems emerge:** test long content, no matches, invalid input, or keyboard recovery. Do not invent findings.
**If finished early:** seek intended-user/client feedback through your agreed contact process.
**Before session 11:** choose meaningful behaviours to automate from your acceptance criteria and defect history.
