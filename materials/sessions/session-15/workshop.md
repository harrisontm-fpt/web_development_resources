# Session 15 workshop — Rehearse and verify readiness

[Briefing and support notes](lecture.md) · [Session index](../README.md)

**180-minute studio · Ungraded readiness check**

## Before you start

Bring the candidate, review action list, test results, README, demonstration data, and draft feedback form. Know the published date/time/timezone for final delivery.

## Step 1 — Triage (0–15 minutes)

Choose the few actions with the greatest effect on final delivery. Identify remaining blockers, minimum requirements, essential defects, and polish. Assign owners and verifiers. Keep optional features frozen.

## Step 2 — Resolve priority issues (15–75 minutes)

Make targeted changes and verify them with the original reproduction plus relevant regression checks. Use lecturer support when stuck, providing evidence of the failure and attempted diagnosis.

Update the deployed candidate and note its commit. Save complete test results rather than reporting an unfinished run as passed.

## Step 3 — Independent README check (75–105 minutes)

A sends one member to B, B to C, C to A. The visitor follows the host team's README while hosts observe without coaching.

Check:

1. Can you understand the purpose and audience?
2. Can you open the application and find its major features?
3. Can you follow setup and testing instructions?
4. Are prerequisites, database preparation, and variable names clear?
5. Can you access evidence and feedback instructions?

Begin setup in an isolated working copy where feasible. Record the exact point reached if it cannot finish in 30 minutes. Distinguish completed execution from instructions merely read. Return to your own team at minute 105.

## Step 4 — Rehearse as a complete team (105–135 minutes)

Run a timed nine-minute presentation, with every member participating. Use realistic demo data and the intended presentation route. Spend approximately five minutes asking the technical questions from the briefing notes.

Use the rest of the block to tighten the demo and record corrections. A recording or a teammate's timing notes can reveal long transitions; you do not need polished slides.

## Step 5 — Correct and check readiness (135–170 minutes)

Fix priority README/demo issues. Verify the feedback form contains the three required 1–5 questions, open comments, and at least one custom question from the [delivery guide](../../reviews_and_delivery.md). Check the lecturer's access to results.

Confirm public access, setup/testing instructions, at least five integration and five E2E tests, design/feedback evidence, contribution records, and AI acknowledgements. Link evidence from the README.

## Step 6 — Record final actions (170–180 minutes)

Each member states their remaining responsibility before session 16. Record unresolved limitations honestly. Confirm the final deadline is 48 hours after session 16 ends.

## Evidence to keep

- [ ] Review actions completed or assigned.
- [ ] Independent README findings and corrections.
- [ ] Timed presentation below 10 minutes with all members participating.
- [ ] Current candidate and quality results.
- [ ] Ready feedback form and accessible results link.
- [ ] Final readiness checklist and outstanding actions.

**If something remains blocked:** prioritise restoring the required journey/public access and identify the exact next action. Document limitations rather than hiding them.
**If finished early:** test a direct route/reload, recovery state, or fresh local setup; avoid destabilising the application with new features.
